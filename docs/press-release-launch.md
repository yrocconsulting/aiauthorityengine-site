## Cory Long Launches AI Authority Engine, an AI SEO Certification Teaching Marketers How to Get Businesses Recommended by ChatGPT

*New certification program trains marketers and agency owners in answer engine optimization as customers shift from search engines to AI assistants*

**[CITY, STATE] — [DATE]** — Entrepreneur, author, and business coach Cory Long today announced the launch of **AI Authority Engine**, an online AI SEO certification and training program that teaches marketers, consultants, and agency owners how to get businesses found, trusted, and recommended by AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Perplexity.

As more consumers ask AI assistants which businesses to hire or which products to buy, those assistants typically name only a handful of companies. AI Authority Engine teaches the skills behind that selection process, including answer engine optimization (AEO), generative engine optimization (GEO), entity SEO, structured data, and AI visibility audits, along with the business systems needed to start and grow an AI SEO agency.

- How AI answer engines identify, evaluate, and recommend businesses
- Answer engine optimization and writing content AI systems cite
- Entity consistency, structured data, and authority signals
- Running AI visibility audits for clients
- How to start an AI SEO agency and get clients

Graduates earn the **Certified AI Authority Specialist** credential. The program is delivered online and is designed for beginners as well as experienced SEO professionals and agency owners.

Long brings more than two decades of digital marketing experience to the program. He is the creator of Digital Storefronts, host of the E-Forge Podcast, and author of *To Love Is To Give*.
